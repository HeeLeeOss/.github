### 🤲 Everything produced here is permanently open

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HeeLeeOss/.github/main/assets/divider.svg" width="100%" height="6" alt=""/></p>

All output published in **Hee-Lee Oss projects** carries an open license — **CC BY 4.0** for content, prose, and educational material; **MIT or CC0** for code and data.

When a contributor works on **another project's repository**, the work is contributed under *that* project's existing license and contribution terms — we do not, and cannot, impose ours on someone else's repo. What we control is which repositories we work on at all, and that is where the bar is applied.

This is not a formality. It means:

- **Anyone can use it, forever** — a teacher in Sayreville, a researcher in Seoul, a government health ministry, a student with a phone and no library. No permission needed, no fee, no expiry.
- **The license is irrevocable** — once published here, the work cannot be re-restricted or taken back. It belongs to the commons permanently.
- **Derivatives stay open** — CC BY requires attribution, so every adaptation, translation, or product built on this work carries the open chain forward. Your contribution multiplies with every downstream use.
- **We check before we contribute** — every repository is screened for its license before any capacity is spent on it. We flag projects that require a Contributor License Agreement (which can let an owner relicense contributions later) or that carve out a proprietary "enterprise" edition, because neither guarantees the work stays open to everyone.
- **The impact compounds over time** — a patient guide translated today can be legally incorporated into a clinic's materials, a government app, a printed handbook, a school curriculum, in five years or fifty years, without anyone needing to track down an author for permission.

A single deed, merged once, can reach people across the world for decades. That is what the license makes possible.

### 📬 Maintainers: ask for capacity

<p align="center"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HeeLeeOss/.github/main/assets/divider.svg" width="100%" height="6" alt=""/></p>

You don't have to move your project here. If you maintain an open project that serves the public good and you're short of hands, add **one file** to your own repository:

```yaml
# .github/heeleeoss.yml
version: "1"
project: your-project
beneficiary: who is actually helped when this ships
definitionOfShipped: your bar for done — merged? released? deployed?
reviewers: [your-github-handle]     # required
license: MIT
riskTier: low
needs:
  - id: docs-001
    title: Translate the getting-started guide into Spanish
    objective: ...
    deliverable: document
    acceptanceCriteria:
      - Reviewed by a Spanish-speaking maintainer
```

Merging that file is the whole opt-in. Then:

- Contributors pick up the needs you listed and open **ordinary pull requests** for you to review or close. Nothing merges without you.
- **`reviewers` is required, and it isn't a formality.** We don't start work on a need that has nobody to review it — an unreviewed PR is a cost to you, not a gift.
- We never get access to your repository, never run anything in your CI, and never act before you merge the file.
- **Delete the file and it stops.** No notice required.
- Every PR we open says up front that it was prepared with AI assistance, names the agent, and links the issue it answers.
- `riskTier: high` (health, legal, safety) requires `expertReview: true` and credentialed sign-off before anything merges.

You may also receive a small pull request from us that adds this file for you, pre-filled from the issues you've already labelled `help wanted`. It changes one file and nothing else. **Close it and we won't ask again** — we keep a permanent do-not-contact record.

We never send unsolicited work, and we never contact a project whose contributing guide declines AI-generated or unsolicited pull requests.

Full documentation, options, and per-agent setup guides are in **[Hee-Lee Oss Downloads](https://github.com/HeeLeeOss/hee-lee-oss-downloads)**.
